🌾 Fertilizer Information

Learn about common fertilizers and their uses in farming.

🌱 Urea

Urea is a nitrogen fertilizer commonly used to support healthy plant growth.

🌾 DAP

DAP provides nitrogen and phosphorus, which are important nutrients for crops.

🍃 NPK

NPK fertilizers contain nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium in different proportions.

🌿 Organic Fertilizer

Organic fertilizers can add nutrients and organic matter to the soil.
